## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Producer → warehouse edge | A load may only move to a warehouse when the road network actually connects the producer's road tile to the warehouse's road tile; no off-road (Manhattan) delivery may occur |
| Commercial Center designation | Only one warehouse may be designated; a second designation request must fall back with a warning and never replace the current designation |
| Commercial Center full | A full designated center must not cause a silent discard — delivery resolves to an accepting alternative with a warning, or is held with a warning |
