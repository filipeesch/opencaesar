## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Per-market config → acceptance | A market must resolve each product by its configured rules and the block-wine-for-plebeians rule per resident class; refusal must gate, defaults must accept except wine-for-plebeians |
| Reservation → availability | A load reserved during transit must be unavailable to a second buyer until released/expired; expiry must be deterministic (tick-based) and never wall-clock |
| Distribution priority | Essential food must be prioritized over the evolution-blocking good, and houses must be ordered by the market's configured service policy — no silent reordering |
