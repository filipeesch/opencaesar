<objective>
Implement the complete, visual, deterministic, inspectable food supply chain as a
vertical slice: production (wheat/vegetables/orchard/meat/fish farms), physical
loads moved by carriers, granary storage with per-food orders and reservations,
market demand/buyers/sellers, house inventory + daily consumption + food variety
evolution, import/export, and the management UI (HUD indicator, advisor, overlays,
alerts) that explains every bottleneck.

Purpose: Turn the food system into the reference for the project's core value —
physical, road-delivered goods with zero teleporting stock and zero invented UI
values. This plan imports the external spec `game-specs/fodd-supply-chain.md` into
GSD format and maps its recommended implementation fases (§34) onto tasks.
Output: `.planning/phases/05-agriculture-food/05-01-PLAN.md` plus the tasks below.
</objective>
